`astronaut.png` is the public-domain NASA astronaut image distributed with
[scikit-image 0.25.2](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/v0.25.2/skimage/data/astronaut.png).
The [scikit-image documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.20.x/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut)
states that the NASA image has no known copyright restrictions and is in the public domain.
