# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IT specialists use MTCH to present their skills, experience, work preferences, and availability. Employers use it to find relevant specialists and contact them with opportunities.

## Product Purpose

MTCH is a reverse-hiring service for IT. Specialists publish profiles; employers select search criteria, review a ranked feed, and send offers directly. The profile flow should make it clear what information is required and when changes become public.

## Brand Personality

Direct, confident, and practical. The interface should feel focused and trustworthy while users create or update a professional profile.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic job-board and SaaS-dashboard templates, decorative complexity, and visual treatments that obscure the profile task.

## Design Principles

- Make each profile step easy to scan and complete.
- Keep drafts private until the user finishes and saves the profile.
- Show only the profile information that is relevant to the current step.
- Keep profile and contact visibility consistent with the existing access rules.
- Preserve a clear keyboard-operable path through the form.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-operable controls and maintain WCAG AA contrast. Associate labels with their fields and communicate validation feedback to assistive technology.
